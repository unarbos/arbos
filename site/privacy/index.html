Arbos

Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Arbos is a Mac and iPhone app that gives each of your projects an always-on agent on your own cloud machine. This page says what we store, where it lives, and how to reach us. It is written to be read, not to cover every case; if something is unclear, ask.

What we store about you

  • Your GitHub identity. When you sign in with GitHub we keep your GitHub user id, login, display name, and avatar URL. We do not receive your GitHub password, and we ask only for the read:user scope.
  • Your devices. A name and id for each Mac or iPhone you sign in from, so you can see and revoke them.
  • A credits ledger. Usage credit granted to your account and the amount each model call and machine-hour used, so we can meter the beta and show you a balance.

Where your chats and files live

Your chats, project files, memory, and history live on your own cloud machine, a sandbox that only your account can reach. Projects on that machine are separate workspaces. The relay that connects your apps to the machine passes messages through and keeps the sync state needed to show the same thread on every device; it does not use your content for anything else. When you delete a project or your account, the machine and its data are removed.

Model calls

The agent's model calls are proxied by our relay to third-party model providers (currently OpenRouter and OpenAI). The text of a request goes to the provider under our account; providers see that content under their own API terms, which exclude API traffic from model training. We log the token counts and cost of each call for the credits ledger, not the content.

Connectors and the vault

If you connect a service such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, or GitHub, the access and refresh tokens are held by the relay, encrypted at rest, and never copied onto the machine. Logins and keys you put in the credential vault are encrypted at rest and injected as placeholders; the model sees masked values. Anything that would leave the machine through a connector is shown to you first as an approval.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not train models on your chats or files.
  • We do not read your machine unless you ask us to help with a specific problem.

Open source

The apps, the relay, and the machine image are public at github.com/unarbos/arbos, so you can check how all of this works or run it yourself.
