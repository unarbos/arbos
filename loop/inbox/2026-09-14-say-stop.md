# `say mode=stop` — PR #163, branch `cursor/say-stop-b027` (on `main`)

T3-09. A parent (any ancestor) ends a worker's turn now: jobs killed, turn interrupted with "stopped by <parent>: <text>", the worker's streamed words written, and the `[done]` to the parent reads "stopped: … / Last words before the stop: …". An idle worker just gets the words as a note. A non-descendant target is refused.

Scenarios:
- mt-style: root spawns a worker that runs a long bash; root `say to=w1 mode=stop` → worker `interrupted` within a few seconds; root's done has the partial words; `kernel.log` `turn_stopped_by_parent`.
- Sibling tries `say to=<sibling> mode=stop` → refused ("not a worker of yours").
- Stop while the worker is between tool calls (model call in flight) → "stopped … during model call" and no partial words (nothing streamed yet).
- The user's own Stop button now also yields a done with "Last words before the stop" when the worker had said something.
- Desktop: the worker's row shows the interrupted line as before; nothing new on the wire.
