# Rig audit: where the symmetry rig can report a pass it has not established, or miss a difference it cannot see

Asked for after the benchmark loop's withdrawn headline (2026-09-17). The rig is `desktop/qa/parity/` (`ui_pass.py` the gate, `journey.py` the acceptance journey, `rig.py` the display pulse) driving the app through `desktop/driver/arbosdriver.py`, plus the by-eye pairing of stills against live Cursor. Each row: the blind spot, what it hid or could hide, what is done, what is not.

| R1 | **Existence read as visibility.** `ids()`/`exists(id)` list every laid-out element, including ones scrolled out of the viewport or under the composer. | R14 | **Assertions that cannot fail.** `self.diff(a, b) or "clicked"` passes on no change; a bare f-string (`f"{n} rows"`) is truthy at n=0. | Found by the deliberate pass over all 119 `check`s (`internal/gate-assertions-what-each-reads.md`): five diff-or-string asserts, three bare f-strings — eight rows that had never been able to fail. | **Fixed** (cycle 31): they return `unverified: …` and record so. Rule: an `ok` lambda returns `True`, `False`, or a string that starts with `unverified:`; a bare description is not a verdict. |

## The rule that falls out

A row may say **pass** only when it has read the thing it names, from the app's state or geometry, and only when that thing is on screen. Existence, a neighbour's movement, an accepted action, and a still nobody compares are evidence, not proof. Rows that cannot meet that are `unverified` or `gap`, and the gate line prints those counts beside the passes — as it does now.
