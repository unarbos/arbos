Your projects, run as agents.

One long-lived agent per project. It delegates the work, talks with you in full duplex, and keeps its state in files you can read, commit, and rewind.

It delegates. The main chat hands each piece of work to a smaller agent and never blocks.

You talk to it. Call a project from your Mac or your phone. Both sides speak and listen at once.

It lives in files. Agents, notes, and plans are plain files in .arbos/, so git saves and rewinds them.
