Here's a list for 3 days of surfing:

**Surf gear**
- Board(s) and board bag, plus a spare fin set and fin key
- Leash, with a spare
- Wax and a wax comb
- Rash guard or surf tee, plus boardshorts (2 pairs so one can dry)
- Ding repair kit

**Sun and skin**
- Reef-safe sunscreen and zinc for your face
- Surf hat or cap and sunglasses
- After-sun or aloe

**Clothes**
- 3 tees, 1 long sleeve for evenings, and shorts
- Underwear and socks
- Sandals
- Light rain jacket

**Essentials**
- Quick-dry towel and a changing poncho
- Reusable water bottle and snacks
- Small first-aid kit (bandages, antiseptic, ear drops)
- Phone, charger, and a waterproof pouch
- Cash, ID, and a key lock box or hidden key spot for while you're in the water

Want me to tweak it for a specific spot or for flying with boards?
