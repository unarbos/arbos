## The review list (design doc, "What does the pass prove?"), the short form

1. Run it against a control that must fail, and read *why* it failed.
2. Ask what else could make it pass (content addressing, a mock echoing the input, a skip scored green, a wait timing out into the expected state, the harness's own copy).
3. Ask what else could make it fail (the shim, a FIFO where a directory belongs, a probe that stages the wrong state).
4. A probe must fail the way the world fails, not a way of its own.
5. When a message changes, ask who reads it.
6. A pass in under a second is a question, not a relief.
